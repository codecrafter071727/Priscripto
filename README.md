# 🏥 Priscripto – Simplified All-in-One Healthcare Platform

Priscripto is a simplified **all-in-one healthcare platform** designed to make healthcare services more accessible and efficient. It allows users to book doctor appointments, check symptoms, receive medicine reminders, and find nearby hospitals in one platform.

The goal of Priscripto is to **simplify healthcare management** through a clean, user-friendly, and efficient digital healthcare solution.

---

## 🚀 Features

- 📅 **Doctor Appointment Booking** – Easily schedule consultations with doctors.
- 🤖 **AI Symptom Checker** – Analyze symptoms and get possible health insights.
- 💊 **Medicine Reminders** – Helps users remember their medications.
- 🏥 **Nearby Hospitals & Bed Availability** – Quickly locate hospitals and check availability.
- 🎨 **Simple and Clean Interface** – Designed for easy navigation and better user experience.

---

## 🎯 User-Centered Design

Priscripto follows **User-Centered Design principles** to ensure a smooth and intuitive experience.  
The platform focuses on:

- Simple and easy-to-use interface  
- Quick access to important features  
- Reduced confusion and minimal steps  
- Efficient healthcare management for users
